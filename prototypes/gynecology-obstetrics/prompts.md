# Gynecology and obstetrics visual prompts

All references were generated with the built-in `imagegen` tool on 2026-08-14.
They contain synthetic identities and values and are design references, never
clinical evidence.

## 01 — Prenatal trajectory

High-fidelity prenatal longitudinal workbench that treats gestational time as
a revisable clinical model. Keep mother and fetus distinct but linked. Show
dating source and revision history, prenatal windows, fetal biometry with curve
provenance, morphology completeness including structures not seen, Rh timing,
metabolic and hypertensive surveillance, planned place of birth and transfer
readiness. Use compact scientific typography and explicit missing states.

## 02 — Labor and birth safety

High-fidelity labor and birth workbench centered on a partogram as a shared
surveillance and decision document. Plot dilation, fetal station, contractions
and maternal observations against patterned alert/action lines. Add fetal
signal quality and provenance, rupture timeline, administrations,
decision-to-birth clock, quantified blood loss, hemorrhage preparation,
newborn first-minutes status and transfer readiness.

## 03 — Reproductive health and postpartum

High-fidelity reproductive and postpartum longitudinal archive. Connect
contraception eligibility and rule provenance, cervical screening lifecycle,
pelvic pain patterns, couple-based infertility context, restricted vulnerability
screening, birth summary, postpartum recovery, breastfeeding, mood follow-up
and unresolved referrals. Distinguish reported, observed, imported,
preliminary, validated, amended and unavailable data.
