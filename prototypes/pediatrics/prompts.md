# Pediatrics visual prompts

All references were generated with the built-in `imagegen` tool on 2026-08-14.
They contain synthetic identities and values and are design references, never
clinical evidence.

## 01 — Growth and dose safety

High-fidelity landscape Clinical UI Pediatrics workbench. Treat chronological
age, corrected age and weight freshness as separate clinical facts. Make the
growth trajectory the dominant object, with weight, height and head
circumference percentiles, explicit curve provenance and a medication safety
rail that exposes the mg/kg calculation, maximum daily dose and administrable
volume. Use compact scientific typography, semantic token colors and no title
larger than 24 px. Avoid generic dashboard cards, gradients and real patients.

## 02 — Prevention and triage

High-fidelity pediatric prevention and acute-triage workbench. Combine an
age-aware immunization and catch-up timeline, developmental milestone windows,
screening results including unavailable states, and a narrow acute-triage rail
with age-specific norms, respiratory and hydration observations, red flags and
an explicit disposition task. Carry meaning with wording, icons and patterns,
not color alone. All data must be visibly synthetic.

## 03 — Trajectory, family and transition

High-fidelity chronic-care and family-coordination workbench for a synthetic
adolescent. Keep child, caregiver and confidential adolescent contexts
distinct. Show longitudinal episodes, control assessments, medication and
action-plan maturity, school and social determinants, consent boundaries,
return precautions and transition readiness. Distinguish patient-reported,
caregiver-reported, observed, imported, preliminary and validated information.
