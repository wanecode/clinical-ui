# Prototypes Cardiology UI

Les trois images ont été générées avec le mode intégré du skill `imagegen`. Elles représentent
uniquement des données et des personnes synthétiques. Aucun actif de référence externe n'a été
utilisé.

## 01 — Cardiovascular cockpit

```text
Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: original desktop clinical application prototype, 16:9
Primary request: Create a high-fidelity cardiology workspace called "Vue cardiovasculaire" for a fully synthetic patient. Show a compact longitudinal summary with explicit human decision states, versioned SCORE2 risk, missing-data visibility, vigilances, and next-action cards.
Scene/backdrop: shippable hospital web application on a warm off-white canvas, no device frame
Style/medium: realistic product UI, distinctive editorial clinical design, not concept art; precise grids and accessible hierarchy
Composition/framing: 16:9 desktop screen; narrow patient context rail; dominant central summary; right-side decision ledger; small lower trajectory chart with a clearly adjacent tabular alternative control
Color palette: deep ink, mineral teal, muted coral for critical states, ochre for preliminary states, off-white surfaces; do not rely on color alone
Text (verbatim): "Vue cardiovasculaire", "SYNTHÉTIQUE", "SCORE2 · version 2026.2", "Données manquantes : HDL", "Décision humaine requise", "Voir le tableau", "Préliminaire", "Validé"
Constraints: fictional synthetic data only; use icons, labels, line styles, patterns and text in addition to color; clinically plausible but not a diagnostic claim; curves, projections and thresholds must have visibly different line styles and direct labels; accessible legible type; no logos, trademarks, watermarks, photographs, or patient-identifying information
Avoid: generic blue dashboard, excessive rounded cards, glassmorphism, decorative heart imagery, unlabeled charts, tiny text
```

## 02 — ECG & ambulatory workbench

```text
Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: original desktop cardiology diagnostic-workbench prototype, 16:9
Primary request: Create a high-fidelity clinical signal review workspace called "Atelier ECG & ambulatoire" for a fully synthetic patient. Unify a large ECG trace review area, structured measurements and interpretation, source/provenance, signal quality, plus compact MAPA and Holter summaries.
Scene/backdrop: shippable hospital web application on a pale paper-gray canvas, no device frame
Style/medium: realistic product UI with a technical instrument-panel character and crisp editorial typography, not concept art
Composition/framing: 16:9 desktop screen; large ECG paper grid and waveform occupies the upper-left two-thirds; provenance and interpretation rail on the right; MAPA pressure chart and Holter event strip below; every chart includes a nearby table-view control
Color palette: charcoal, bone, electric cyan accents, signal red, muted violet, and amber; do not rely on color alone
Text (verbatim): "Atelier ECG & ambulatoire", "SYNTHÉTIQUE", "Source brute", "Qualité du signal : acceptable", "Importé", "Interprétation préliminaire", "Validation humaine", "Voir les mesures en tableau", "Appareil indisponible"
Constraints: fictional synthetic data only; ECG paper grid, waveform, measurements, annotations and calibration marker must be clear; observed signals, artifact zones, alert thresholds and clinician annotations use different line styles, hatch patterns, shapes and direct labels; show provenance identifiers without real PHI; use icons and words as well as color; clinically plausible but not a diagnostic claim; no logos, trademarks, watermarks, photographs, or patient-identifying information
Avoid: generic card dashboard, glossy gradients, decorative heart imagery, unreadable ECG traces, color-only events, tiny controls
```

## 03 — Trajectory & safety

```text
Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: original desktop longitudinal cardiology care prototype, 16:9
Primary request: Create a high-fidelity care-coordination workspace called "Trajectoire & sécurité" for a fully synthetic patient. Center the screen on a braided longitudinal timeline connecting symptoms, echocardiography, prescriptions, implanted device checks, follow-up actions, reports and vigilance alerts.
Scene/backdrop: shippable hospital web application on a soft warm-gray canvas, no device frame
Style/medium: realistic product UI with a calm archival/timeline aesthetic, inspired by a well-designed clinical casebook rather than a generic dashboard
Composition/framing: 16:9 desktop screen; horizontal multi-lane timeline dominates center; prescription safety matrix in a left rail; implanted-device timeline and report lifecycle in a right rail; bottom vigilance board with actionable ownership; include table alternative for the trajectory
Color palette: graphite, parchment, forest green, rust, indigo, and safety red; do not rely on color alone
Text (verbatim): "Trajectoire & sécurité", "SYNTHÉTIQUE", "Prescription à confirmer", "Interaction potentielle", "Dispositif implanté", "Compte rendu amendé", "Signé", "Critique", "Responsable : Dr Synthèse", "Voir la chronologie en tableau"
Constraints: fictional synthetic data only; distinguish observed events, imported data, derived trends, projections, preliminary reports, amendments, signed reports and critical alerts using icon shapes, labels, borders, patterns and typography in addition to color; preserve explicit clinician ownership and human confirmation; show a clinically plausible device follow-up but no real manufacturer logo or patient identifiers; accessible legible type; no logos, trademarks, watermarks, photographs, or decorative heart imagery
Avoid: generic card grid, rainbow palette, unlabeled timeline dots, color-only status, glossy visual effects, tiny text, autonomous treatment recommendations
```
