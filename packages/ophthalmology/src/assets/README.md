# Synthetic OCT Storybook assets

These images are generated, non-diagnostic teaching fixtures. They do not contain patient data and
must not be presented as real clinical imaging.

Generation mode: built-in `imagegen`, one generation per asset.

## `oct-od-current-synthetic.png`

> Scientific educational synthetic retinal OCT B-scan asset for an ophthalmology Storybook fixture. Landscape single cross-sectional macular OCT scan, right eye, current visit, plausible layered retinal anatomy with a clear central foveal depression and subtle natural RPE contour variation, moderate-to-high signal quality. Grayscale clinical imaging aesthetic on a pure black background, fine granular speckle, crisp but not overly polished, anatomically coherent. Image content only: no UI frame, no labels, no measurement scale, no text, no logo, no watermark. This must look explicitly like a simulated educational scan rather than identifiable patient data. Wide 16:9 composition, retinal tissue centered with generous black space above and below.
