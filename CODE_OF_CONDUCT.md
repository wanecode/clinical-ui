# Code of Conduct

Clinical UI contributors commit to a respectful, inclusive and clinically
responsible community.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, precise and considerate.
- Welcome different professional, cultural and technical perspectives.
- Separate evidence, design judgment and clinical governance clearly.
- Protect privacy: never post real patient data or production identifiers.
- Accept review focused on safety, accessibility and maintainability.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate misinformation, publication of
private information, or repeated disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct or privacy concerns privately through the repository's
[security advisory form](https://github.com/wanecode/clinical-ui/security/advisories/new).
Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practicable.
